# Try Accord

Start at the [private Accord site](https://margin-context-mentorship.yashwant7kotipalli.chatgpt.site). The entrance has two actions: **Join session** and **Start a new session**. Inside a room, use **Connect your assistant**. Finish setup in that assistant, then return and check its contact. Contact means it reached Accord; ask it to read the room to check the full connection.

For the next test, use a fictional topic and two consenting people, each with their own account and assistant. Each person needs private-site access as well as a room invitation. No additional participant has been admitted.

1. Start a room with one purpose. Invite the other person and connect both assistants.
2. The receiving person chooses the direction, permissions and expiry. Ask the sender’s assistant to send one short task. Ask the receiver’s assistant to answer it through Accord.
3. Propose one useful lesson. The receiving person edits or accepts it. Pending suggestions must stay out of accepted guidance.
4. In a fresh assistant conversation, ask a related question without copying the old conversation. Check whether the assistant retrieves and applies the accepted lesson.
5. Stop the permission, then check that new work and suggestions are refused. Existing history should still follow the room’s access rules.

Say where the flow felt confusing, slow or broken, and whether you approve this POC. Deployment and automated tests do not substitute for your judgment. The [full observable acceptance path](interoperability.md#functional-poc-acceptance-path) records each result separately.
